Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 Terms of Use →

This policy explains how the TrigoCalc Android app ("TrigoCalc", "the app"), published by Dawn Routine ("we", "us"), handles information. TrigoCalc does not require an account, and we do not run servers that collect your personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your phone. We do not receive it.

  • Calculation history: the calculations you complete, if "Save calculations" is turned on in Settings. You can turn this off or clear the history at any time in Settings.
  • Settings: your theme, decimal precision and history preferences.
  • Premium status: whether you have purchased TrigoCalc Premium.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of TrigoCalc shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data related to ads

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).
  • Remove ads: TrigoCalc Premium removes all ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

TrigoCalc Premium is purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment. We never receive your card or payment details. The app only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can unlock Premium. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms for how Google handles payment information.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives information through the app is Google, as described in sections 2 and 3. Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit.

5. Children

TrigoCalc is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you clear it in the app's Settings or uninstall the app. We do not hold any of your data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's controls at My Ad Center.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, most requests are handled through the in-app controls and Google's tools described above. For any privacy question, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
