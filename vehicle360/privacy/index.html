Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 Terms of Use →

This policy explains how the Vehicle 360 Android app ("Vehicle 360", "the app"), published by Dawn Routine ("we", "us"), handles information. Vehicle 360 does not require an account, and we do not run servers that collect or store your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not receive it.

  • Vehicle records: your vehicles, fuel and charging entries, expenses, service and maintenance records, reminders and documents.
  • Photos: pictures of receipts and documents (for example a driving licence, RC or insurance policy) that you choose to attach.
  • Settings and Premium status: your preferences, and whether you have Vehicle 360 Premium.

This data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Vehicle 360, so nothing is copied to your Google account or to us.

Your data can be lost

Because there is no cloud copy, uninstalling the app, clearing its data or changing phones deletes all your records unless you have made a backup. Please make backups regularly. The app reminds you to.

2. Camera, photos and files

The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo of a receipt or document, and opens your photo library only when you choose to attach an existing picture. Photos are stored on your device and are never uploaded by the app.

3. Backups and reports

  • Backups are ZIP files that you create and then choose where to save or share (for example Google Drive or email). Dawn Routine never receives them.
  • Backups contain sensitive files. A backup includes your document photos, such as your driving licence and RC, and it is not encrypted or password-protected. Store and share it carefully, as you would the original documents.
  • Reports (such as PDF exports) are created on your phone and are shared only when you tap Share.

4. Notifications

Reminders for insurance, service, documents and similar items are generated on your device. No reminder data is sent to us.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Vehicle 360 shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data related to ads

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Your vehicle records are never shared with AdMob. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from More → Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).
  • Remove ads: Vehicle 360 Premium removes all ads.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

Vehicle 360 Premium is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play. Google processes the payment. We never receive your card or payment details. The app only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can unlock Premium. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms for how Google handles payment information.

7. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives information through the app is Google, as described in sections 5 and 6. Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. Anything else leaves your phone only when you choose to share a backup or report.

8. Children

Vehicle 360 is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

9. Data retention and deletion

All your vehicle data is stored locally on your device. We do not store any of it on our servers.

How to delete your data

  • Delete all data from the app: open More → Settings → Delete All Data. This permanently removes all vehicles, fuel and charging entries, expenses, maintenance records, reminders, documents, attached photos and app settings from your device.
  • Uninstall the app: uninstalling Vehicle 360 removes all app data from your device.

Both actions are immediate and cannot be undone.

Backups and advertising data

Backups you have previously saved (for example to Google Drive or your files) are under your control and must be deleted separately. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's controls at My Ad Center.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, most requests are handled through the in-app controls and Google's tools described above. For any privacy question, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
